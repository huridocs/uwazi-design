Research tools on Nepal: a review tour

The OSINT workflow on the Nepal protests collection, in fourteen steps. Each step shows one surface at 1440 (light) and says which question it answers; the numbers refer to the twenty questions in gap-analysis.md.

Branch f5-on-f7-wip at 32bae2de: playground db2c6a44 (F1–F4, F6, F7, Content card, drawer Filters tab), F5 cherry-picked on top (78d44197, 882d7741, ba9ea163), and the saved-views fix below. Headless Chromium, light theme only; dark mode was not checked. 6 October 2026.

Before the tour: saved views keep F5's state

F5 replaced the Country and Descriptor AND/OR switches with a Match mode per facet and added ranges and groups, so F7's atoms/savedViews.ts no longer compiled. Commit 32bae2de puts match, ranges and groups in the LibrarySnapshot, writes them back on apply, and counts them in snapshotFilterCount the way the filter badge does. The keys are optional: a view or link saved before F5 opens with the defaults, and an old "AND" opens as all.

Checked on Nepal with Casualty record, Verification none of Disputed, Age 18–30 and an OR group of Cause and Age (25 results, five chips): saved view after a reload, search history after a reload, and the #view= link in a fresh load all restore the same 25 results, chips, Match mode, bounds and group. An old-format view opens with defaults and is marked current. tsc and build pass.

  1. The collection and the Content card

    2,124 records on the 2024–2026 protests. The Content card counts what records carry: 51 with a document, 29 images, 50 videos, 25 audio. "More" opens where it is stored, language, length and quoted passages.

    Starting point for every question.

  2. Event › Where = Kathmandu District

    The Where chain filters events by the place they happened at, or any place that place is in. Kathmandu District returns 85 events, including those tied only to a building inside it. The Who chain below narrows the same set by person or organisation.

    Questions 7 and 8, place half.

  3. Match modes and an OR group

    Each facet has a Match row (any, none, missing); Age is a range. Casualty records, Verification none of Disputed, and a group joining Cause = Burns or fire with Age 18–30 by OR: 25 records. The group shows as one chip, "Cause as reported or Age".

    Questions 4, 5 and 18.

  4. A date and hour range, on the brush and the map

    Events in Kathmandu District from 12:00 on 8 September to 18:00 on 9 September: 45, matched when the event's span overlaps the range. The brush and the map read the same filter; 42 are located, 3 have no geolocation.

    Question 7.

  5. A timed event and "Why we believe this"

    "Police fire on protesters at Parliament and New Baneshwar" is timed 12:37 to 16:00. Every field shows how many sources stand behind it. On When, the popover lists the six sources, marks the value Disputed and says the start time and the order are contested.

Defects and rough edges seen on the way

Not fixed. wrong gives a wrong answer, data is in the corpus or its build, ui is presentation.

  1. wrongdataAs of answers wrongly. Ministry of Home Affairs, As of 2025-09-09, held office at: Ramesh Lekhak "since 2024". Ref bg24-411 is an open-ended copy of the tenure that 8sep-112 closes on 2025-09-08, so the open one wins. The build should close or merge duplicate tenures.
  2. uiHours are never shown. The event above is timed 12:37–16:00 and "Time precision" reads Hour, but When shows "2025/09/08 – 2025/09/08". Cards show "09/09/2025 – 09/09/2025" for one day and "05/10/2026 –" with a dangling dash.
  3. uiFive date formats on one screen (step 4): 2025/09/08 in the field, "2025-09-08 12:00 → 2025-…" in the truncated chip, "8 Sep 2025, 12:00" on the brush, "12:00 PM" in the 12-hour time input, 09/09/2025 on cards.
  4. uiThe map and brush do not fit the filter. With Kathmandu District and a 30-hour range the map opens on all of northern India (one click on the cluster to reach Kathmandu), and the range is a 2px sliver on a quarterly brush.
  5. ui"Why we believe this" covers the value it explains, and its note cites claims by slug (first-shots-at-12-37, peter-one-radio-order-12-40) instead of titles or links.
  6. uiThe drawer opens on an empty Document tab ("No document") for records with no file once another record set it (Lamichhane, the claim, the Ministry), leaving half the screen blank.
  7. uiThe Relationships view is global. Graph chosen on Lamichhane opened the claim in graph at 217%, which hides the claim's Evidence block; the block shows only in List.
  8. uiConnect to: the search field is not focused when the dialog opens. Path 2 runs through the "Kathmandu" location hub, the trivial path the gap analysis warned about; the gap analysis measured 3 hops, the tool finds 4.
  9. uiGraph path: the label "NHRC submits report recomme…" sits on top of the "targets" group label; the highlighted edges carry no relation names.
  10. dataClaim status against its matrix. "2,660 verified injured" and "2,522 wounded per the commission report" are Disputed yet "none disputes it". "Two of the 19 deaths on 8 September were in Jhapa" carries figure 19, Nationwide, not 2 in Jhapa.
  11. uiEvidence view has no figure sort. It lists by Date added, so 2,000 injured, 2,660 injured, 19 killed, 2,522 injured follow each other; the meta line wraps as "Nationwide ·" with the separator at the line end.
  12. uiCitations drop the stance. The claim's disputing quotes ("killing 17 people") are listed as citations for "19 killed" with nothing saying they dispute it.
  13. uiAdd group starts on the wrong members. It picks the first two narrowing facets ("Type or Verification"), so both pickers need changing. A value the current results lack cannot be ticked, so Burns had to be ticked before Age narrowed the list.
  14. uiPDF jump is page-level. The quoted passage on page 7 is not highlighted, and the reference label and the "No translation in EN. Showing NE." notice cover the top lines of the page.
  15. uiSearch highlights inside words. Query words "on" and "at" mark "water", "cannon", "Kathmandu", "International".
  16. uiSearch history doubles quotes for a quoted query: “"Ministry of Home Affairs"”.
  17. uiPhone: Filters is behind the bottom bar's "…" menu; the toolbar has no filter control. A quote in the claim matrix leaves "8.”" alone on its line.
  18. uiVerification is not the leading facet, as CLAUDE.md says it is: it comes after Status, Template and Content.
