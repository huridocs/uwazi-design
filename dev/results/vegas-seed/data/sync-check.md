# Vegas seed: sync check

Generated by `data/sync_report.py` from the map's own annotations (nothing external except the comparisons in sections 3 and 4). Times are Las Vegas local time (PDT) on 1 October 2017. A volley annotation is a line such as `10:13:31 10th Volley Begins :02` on a recording: the clock time of the start of the volley, and the offset in the video.

Annotations marked partial on the map (for example `(last :02)` or `(:03 in)`) are left out of the volley statistics: the video starts inside the volley, so the clock time is not the volley's start. 29 annotations are marked this way, 27 of them volleys; the statistics below use the other 482 volley annotations.
