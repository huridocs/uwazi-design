I am working on a research prototype that tests tools for lining up recordings of an event on one clock and analysing them. The 1 October 2017 Las Vegas shooting is our test case, and your map is the most complete record of the recordings that we found.

On 7 October 2026 we downloaded the map's KML export once and used its annotations: clock times, the offsets of the twelve volleys inside each video, camera positions and paths, and the venue features.
