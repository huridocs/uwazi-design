Press Release

Bhadra 27, 2082 (September 12, 2025)

A solution should be sought based on human rights, democratic values, and the rule of law

Amid the current critical situation in the country, the National Human Rights Commission is in
communication with the Rt.Hon. President Mr. Ram Chandra Poudel.

The Commission has conveyed to the President that it would be appropriate to find a solution
grounded in the principles of human rights, democratic values, and the rule of law.

The Commission holds a firm belief that the President will take the appropriate steps to protect
human rights, democracy, the establishment of peace and the rule of law in the country.

Additionally, the Commission is in continuous communication with other concerned stakeholders
regarding the current situation.

(Dr. Tikaram Pokharel)
